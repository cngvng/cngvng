## About

I am a **Research Engineer at VNPT AI** and a **Research Assistant at the Applied AI Lab, Phenikaa University**, working with [Dr. Luong Van Thien](https://tvluong.wordpress.com/) and [Dr. Mai Xuan Trang](https://comp.phenikaa-uni.edu.vn/faculty/dr-mai-xuan-trang/).

I study how foundation and agentic models reason over **multimodal, temporal, and structured evidence**. My work connects multi-agent language systems, knowledge graphs, and patient representation learning, with healthcare as a primary application domain.
